YOUR BOOKS, AT YOUR PACE

A place for the
page and the voice.

Dogma brings reading and saved narration into a local desktop library. The application is in active development. This listening room offers a first taste of its voice.

01

Bring your own books

Import EPUB and FB2, browse chapters, read, and keep your place with bookmarks.

02

Return to a familiar voice

Prepare narration with a reviewed cast, then listen to saved passages and resume where you left off.

03

Keep listening your way

Export saved audio as MP3 or M4B, with caption formats available for reading alongside it.
