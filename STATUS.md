# Current implementation status — 0.1.19

This is a source implementation, not a verified offline installer. The library schema is unchanged; existing books, frozen casts, ready generations and accepted audio stay valid.

## Implemented in source

| Reader | Chapter-based reading, bookmarks, internal links, appearance settings, inline paragraph illustrations and source-anchor navigation. |

## Important boundaries

- Nothing in the current source was validated with a real text or speech model, a native or signed build, or Windows. That includes converting the response schema, with its passage, unit, speaker and character ID enums, into a llama-cpp-python grammar. The gold-set evaluation harness exists, but no real-model score has been recorded.
- Analysis covers the primary book text plus selected optional sections even for a chapter-only rendering request, and it is not reused by later generations of the same book. Each request carries at most 40 relevant character registry entries, fewer when the context cannot hold them all. Entries that do not fit with descriptions stay listed compactly (ID, name, ≤3 aliases) and remain valid IDs. Measured 2026-10-05 (Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 Hugging Face tokenizer, not llama.cpp; one Russian novel; 8192 context; synthetic entries): compact entry ≈54 tokens, median request lists 27 of 40 and gets no descriptions, 111 of 134 first batches split, median 2,600 target characters per request. Entries are dropped from the least relevant end so that each request keeps room for its full output limit; a batch of several passages is split when the characters named in it or its context and the accepted speakers of its context alone leave less room than that, and a single passage that lacks that room even without entries gets the room that is left. A passage that stays invalid after one re-ask, or does not fit the model alone, is narrated by the narrator voice and listed as degraded instead of failing the generation. After a timeout the worker restarts, and the batch is split or a single passage is asked once more; a second timeout in a row, in analysis or alias comparison, fails the generation. Timeout counts per passage are saved with the generation; on the next run a passage that was in two timed-out requests is sent alone without a timeout retry, and when that request times out after one that did not, the passage is narrated by the narrator voice and listed as degraded. Alias reconciliation runs at most 256 comparisons of name-based candidate pairs; pairs of characters with known opposite genders and different names are not compared, groups that contain such a pair are never joined, and a pair that does not fit or times out once, and pairs beyond that limit, stay unmerged with a notice. A capitalized descriptor yields to a revealed name only when the book writes that exact word form mostly in lowercase and fewer than 3 times in title case mid-sentence; full names of the reconciliation name table and the case forms of its diminutives always count as names.
- Character voices do not need to be globally unique. Local dialogue conflicts are discouraged, but conversation grouping is structural: it bridges one narration paragraph and splits at a second one. Mechanical speakers get mechanical profiles only when the pack declares them. Acoustic differentiation and semantic attribution still require evaluation.
- Cloned delivery without a supported reference variant falls back to neutral with a passage warning; a CustomVoice pack without delivery control produces one generation notice instead. Basic audio validation and the duration plausibility bounds do not prove spoken-word fidelity or acting quality.
- The speech language is pinned per generation when it first renders. A generation from before this version keeps the earlier rule (`Russian` for a declared language starting with `ru`, `English` for `en`, otherwise `Auto`) when it already has stored audio, or when its analysis was completed before this version and audio saved under the earlier key exists for its passages. A passage at take 0 reuses audio saved under the earlier key only when the generation's language equals that earlier rule, or when the pack uses `auto` language mode. A new generation of a book whose language is now chosen differently therefore synthesizes that audio again instead of mixing languages.
- The fine-tuning adapter performs single-speaker adaptation, not multi-narrator or controllable-character/emotion training. It requires the prepared CUDA/BF16/FlashAttention environment; optimizer-state resume and Mac/CPU optimization are not implemented. No trained narrator has been validated.
- Live Codex/image-tool operation is unverified. The CLI must already be installed and signed in. Saved images work offline; starting or retrying online work requires explicit approval.
- Full-book text remains in memory and a complete chapter is mounted. Continuous playback prefetches the next ready passage, but the remaining gap has not been measured. No target-device memory, frame-rate, real-time inference or gapless-audio performance claim is established.
- Cast inspection and the cast review panel can expose future identities. Spoiler-aware character display and guaranteed visual-character consistency are not implemented.
- Native file association handling now accepts startup files, second-instance arguments and macOS open events through one bounded import path. Target-platform installer and lifecycle verification remain required.
- Startup removes only transient leftovers and never deletes book folders or audio that the opened database does not reference. Unreferenced audio is still collected after every finished job, so after an older database is restored, the first finished job removes audio that database does not reference.
- Kept by decision: `.fb2.zip` files are not imported, FB2 XML is parsed strictly, books over 2,000,000 characters of text are refused, and analysis and casting are not reused across generations.

## Not implemented or not included

Model weights, curated narrator voice packs, compiled runtimes and signed installers are not included. Foliate-js, mpv, MLX and Laya integrations, rolling audio/image preparation, book-wide pronunciation dictionaries, modified-EPUB/video export and validated physics tutoring remain unfinished. Russian and English controls are available and casting notices are translated by code; messages that join several known texts are translated part by part, while some prompts and low-level runtime, worker and provider messages remain in English. Study view displays source content and attached images.

Schema 4 separates reading and listening positions and adds bounded per-item revision journals for compact polling while retaining existing source IDs, frozen casts, accepted audio, model fingerprints and stored audio keys. When the selected pack folder no longer contains `pack.json`, as after moving the macOS app or on a new AppImage mount, a background check after startup selects the bundled pack whose fingerprint matches the newest pin among the generations made from that folder. New generations record their pin time and corrections keep it; when no generation made from that folder has one, as for generations made before this version, this rule applies only when they all share one fingerprint. Otherwise, for a stored bundled-pack location it selects the bundled pack of the same folder name, or else the sole bundled pack. Any other missing custom folder stays selected and is reported by the health check. A moved or bundled pack serves a saved generation only when its pinned fingerprint and verified assets match, and pack files changed after verification stop preparation. Packs that declare less than 8192 tokens of text context cannot be used for new analysis, and they can be selected only when they declare at least 4096 tokens and an existing generation is pinned to them, for example after their folder moved; the health check then still reports the context error and new generations stay blocked. Generations already pinned to a pack with 4096 to 8191 tokens keep rendering, repair, cast review and corrections. On Windows the library moves once, as one folder, from `%APPDATA%` to its own `library` folder under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.rimskifikator.desktop`, and stays in `%APPDATA%` when that move is not possible.
